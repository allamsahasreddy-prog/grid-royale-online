How to play

Classic rules
Classic: Take turns placing X and O. The first player to make a line of three wins.
Ultimate: Your move sends your opponent to the mini-board in the same cell position. If that board is already closed, they may choose any open board.
Tip: Winning a mini-board claims that square on the large board. Claim three large-board squares in a row to win.
